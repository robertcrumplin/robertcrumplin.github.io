This excerpt is from the lecture Valuations and Kapranov's theorem in my lecture course on tropical geometry. I define the tropicalisation of a subvariety of the torus over a valued field, compute it for the line x + y + 1 = 0 by considering the possible valuations of x and y, and compare the result with the definition of a tropical curve as a bend locus given earlier in the course.
